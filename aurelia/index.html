The kitchen

Meet the head chef

The best ingredient is patience. We wait for the season to arrive, then cook it simply and with care.

Ilya Moreau

Head Chef & Founder

After training in Lyon and Copenhagen, Ilya Moreau opened Aurelia in 2019 on a quiet Clerkenwell street. The kitchen works with fewer than twenty suppliers, most within a day's drive. Whole animals are butchered in-house, and the bread starter is older than the restaurant.

Inside Aurelia

Rooms made for lingering.

Warm oak, brass and soft light. The rooms are designed so that the evening can run long and a second bottle never feels like a bad idea.

Reserve a table.
